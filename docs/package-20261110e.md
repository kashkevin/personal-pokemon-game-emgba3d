## What changed since run 422's build

The engine's `cb0978fd` (PC only); the app's records only; the GBA ROM unchanged. What a player meets:

* **The saved party count in the Battle Tower's multi partner room (KNOWN_ISSUES MS-234, Low: save integrity, a rare
  state)** (engine `cb0978fd`; found by the UBSan build's sweep of every place in 3D): the room where a multi battle's
  partner is chosen runs the Battle Pyramid's party restore when it resumes (the original game's script), two of the
  three choices made. The empty choice read the saved party at index -1 - the end of the saved map view and the saved
  party count - and, when those bytes were zero, wrote an empty Pokemon over them: the saved party count 0 (the six
  Pokemon themselves untouched). The restore now skips a choice not made, as the game's other party restores do; for
  every choice made it does what it did.
