## What changed since run 448's build

* **The voxel trees' memory (MS-244, the owner's decision #58)** (engine `6e948186`): coarser models for trees far along
  the view and for shadows off the picture, the faces turned away culled - the same picture, about a quarter less memory.
* **Raised tops' corners (MS-248, the owner: "stop adding these blocks as edges")** (engines `88a4b990`, `88a283bc`,
  `21b2e28f`): a corner no one walks on stands as rock sloping to the low ground, not a block a cell square; not down to
  a voxel tree's cell (a pixel of sky in run 454); a chunk the 3D view keeps while walking checks the cells past its
  area the corners read (stale chunks in run 457). Straight edges and the tall grass's squares are the owner's to decide.
* **Terraces levelled by their stairs (MS-247, the owner's picture of Route 119)** (engines `3e03e4ac`, `62b1615c`,
  `c7a50d6d`, `0595e01c`): Route 119's grass bank a level over its rock plateau, its stairs climbing; Mt. Pyre's
  exterior a level a staircase to the summit; Birth Island's top over its landing; Mossdeep City's Space Center two
  flights over the town; the Weather Institute's front and two lawn strips on Route 119 meeting the raised bank (a hole
  before the door and two steps a tile down, from the bank's first build).
* **Navel Rock's sand over its pier (MS-249)** (engines `5c977944`, `3bb0e971`): the courtyard before the cave lay a
  level under the pier; the sand stands a level over it, the stairs climbing to it; a dark block on the rock's west
  side is a face like the five beside it. A faint seam is left beside those faces (two fixes tried, each worse
  elsewhere; KNOWN_ISSUES MS-249).
* **Two native screens (MS-246, MS-250)** (engines `6608cef3`, `78caf633`): the Battle Tower lobby's Apprentice asks its
  questions on the native field text screen; the new game's introduction (Birch's speech, BOY / GIRL, the name's YES /
  NO) on a native screen of its own. Every button stays the game's.
* **The native interface's audit names its addresses on Windows (MS-105)** (engine `840de6dd`).
* The Windows checks: every check of the 3D job from the frame rate on runs whatever an earlier one found; the 3D tour
  37 places (Mt. Pyre's exterior, Birth Island, Navel Rock added); the voxel trees' memory, MS-108's transitions and
  MS-105's screens, the presets and window modes measured.

Play is unchanged: the 3D changes only draw; the two screens draw the game's own choices.
