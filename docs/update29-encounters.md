# Update 29 — wild encounters, Legendary and Mythical spawns, the Safari Zone, the Shiny Charm and the Mega economy

Engine branch `claude/dev-update29-encounters`, app branch `claude/dev-update29-encounters`. Everything below was measured in the cloud (headless, xvfb,
**software OpenGL, no GPU**, no real keyboard, no second PC). Nothing here is a real-PC or real-network result; what was not run is listed at the end.

## 1. Wild levels (part 1)

A wild Pokemon's level has always come from the encounter table of the map and the method (grass, cave floor, surf, fishing, Rock Smash, the Safari
Zone): `ChooseWildMonLevel` picks a level between that slot's minimum and maximum (`src/data/wild_encounters.h`), whatever species ends up in the slot.
The game's species randomizer (`MPWild_RandomizeSpecies`) only swaps the species and keeps that level. This update **does not change any of it**: no level
is scaled to the party, the badges or the story, no level is forced to 1 or raised for rare species, the slot differences (common slots low, rare slots at the top
of the range) stay, and `src/data/wild_encounters.h` is a protected file of the data-integrity check (section 9).

* A random Legendary or Mythical (section 3) takes the **level of the encounter it replaces**: a Rayquaza met on Route 101 is level 2 or 3, as the Zigzagoon it replaced
  would have been; a Mewtwo in the Safari Zone is level 25 to 29 (the Zone's grass table). It is never raised to 50, 70 or 100.
* Measured through the game's own generator (`TryGenerateWildMon`, then the Legendary roll): Route 101 grass 6,000,000 encounters, levels 2-3 for ordinary and Legendary alike;
  Route 105 surfing 3,000,000, levels 5-35; Safari Zone South 3,600,000, levels 25-29 (every level seen is one of the table's own).
* Scripted, static and event encounters (the first Zigzagoon, the starters, Groudon/Kyogre/Rayquaza, Mew and the other fixed Legendaries, trainers) are not random
  encounters: their code path is unchanged, they never roll, and the data-integrity check fails if their data changes.

## 2. Ordinary Pokemon

Unchanged apart from the Safari additions (section 4): the 312 Gen 1-6 evolution families of `src/data/mp_wild_pool.h` are all available on every encounter method
whatever the map (no type or habitat restriction), with four rarity tiers (weights 30/15/6/3 per family), the time-of-day, area-type and weather multipliers of
Update 2, and the level-gated evolution stage (`ChooseMember`). Measured on Route 101: 283 distinct ordinary species in 6,000,000 encounters (21 Fire and 27 Water types).
Legendary and Mythical Pokemon are not in this pool at all; they come only from the separate roll below.

## 3. Random Legendary and Mythical encounters (part 3)

**Odds.** Every random wild encounter rolls once: exactly **1 in 6,000**, one combined chance for the whole pool (not per species), made with integer arithmetic
(`((u64)random * 6000) >> 32 == 0`). Measured: 60,000,000 rolls gave 9,810 hits (expected 10,000 +- 100).

**Pool.** 54 species, built from the game's own classification (`isRestrictedLegendary`, `isSubLegendary`, `isMythical`; the self-test fails if a flagged species
is missing or an unflagged one is present): all sixteen Mythicals (Mew, Celebi, Jirachi, Deoxys, Phione, Manaphy, Darkrai, Shaymin, Arceus, Victini, Keldeo, Meloetta,
Genesect, Diancie, Hoopa, Volcanion), the Kanto/Johto/Hoenn/Sinnoh/Unova/Kalos Legendaries and the trios. Each has habitat tags (generated by
`tools/mp/gen_legend_pool.py`); an encounter's area has tags (its map section, whether it is a cave, water, the weather); a species is eligible when they share a tag
(Darkrai and Cresselia only at night). **Outside the Safari Zone** the habitat rules apply; the area's pool is picked uniformly. No species is unobtainable (every one is eligible
somewhere; no real area has an empty pool; an area that had none would keep its ordinary Pokemon).

**Where it rolls.** Only at the creation of a random wild encounter: grass, cave floor, surfing, fishing, Rock Smash and the walk-into overworld Pokemon. A pending-note
mechanism (species and personality must match what the encounter code just made) keeps DexNav, scripted battles, roamers, outbreaks, Feebas and the Battle Frontier out. The shiny
status of the encounter is kept (a Legendary can be shiny, and a stored shiny is untouched); there is no shiny lock.

**Measured through the real generator** (`--selftest-legend`, 60 checks, 0 failed): Route 101 6M encounters: 1,022 Legendary/Mythical (expected 1,000 +- 32), levels 2-3, all 16 species of its
pool appeared; Route 105 surfing 3M: 465 (expected 500 +- 22), 9 of 9 pool species, Kyogre possible and Groudon not; Safari South 3.6M: 861 (expected 900 +- 30), 54 of 54 species.

## 4. Safari Zone (part 4)

* **Legendary/Mythical:** 1 in 4,000 per encounter (60,000,000 rolls: 15,014 hits, expected 15,000 +- 122), **any** of the 54 species whatever the habitat or the hour, drawn uniformly
  from one pool (5,400,000 draws: each species 99,240-100,620 of an expected 100,000). One roll per encounter, never added to the ordinary 1/6000.
* **Levels** are the Zone's tables (25-31). Safari Balls, the step counter, the ball counter, bait/rock/run and catching are untouched.
* **Additional rare and exclusive Pokemon** (PC game only): eight ordinary families appear only in the Safari Zone: Kangaskhan, Tauros, Happiny (Chansey line), Scyther (Scizor line),
  Pinsir, Heracross, Mime Jr. (Mr. Mime) and Farfetch'd. They have weight 0 outside the Zone (`MP_WILD_SAFARI_EXCLUSIVE`, `src/platform/mp_wild_safari.h`, generated by
  `tools/mp/gen_wild_pool.py`), and every rare and very rare family is three times as likely inside it (`MP_WILD_SAFARI_RARE_BOOST`). Measured: 1,500,000 Route 101 encounters: none of the eight;
  3,000,000 Safari encounters: they appear, as a few percent of encounters. They are obtainable by the intended route (enter the Zone). The shared multiplayer spawns never use them (the Zone is
  not shared).

## 5. Shiny odds and the Shiny Charm (part 5)

Every wild Pokemon gets independent 1/4096 rolls: 1 normally, **+2 with the Shiny Charm** (the engine's Gen 6 Charm), **+1 inside the Safari Zone** (new). The odds are exactly
`1 - (4095/4096)^rolls`:

| Case | Rolls | Odds | Measured (20,000,000 each) |
|---|---:|---|---|
| normal | 1 | 1 in 4096 | 1 in 4131 (4,841 of 4,883 +- 70) |
| Shiny Charm | 3 | 1 in 1365.7 | 1 in 1370 (14,595 of 14,645 +- 121) |
| Safari Zone | 2 | 1 in 2048.3 | 1 in 2042 (9,794 of 9,764 +- 99) |
| Safari Zone + Charm | 4 | 1 in 1024.4 | 1 in 1020 (19,611 of 19,524 +- 140) |

The Charm is a Key Item in the player's own bag (per player, never shared in multiplayer; a bag without it gets nothing). It does not change the Legendary/Mythical probability
(6,000,000 Route 101 encounters with the Charm: 1,008, expected 1,000 +- 32). Existing shinies are unchanged; no shiny lock is added; a Legendary or Mythical can be shiny.

## 6. Game Corner economy and the Mega Evolution unlock (parts 5 and 6)

**The Coin Case now holds up to 999,999 coins** (was 9,999). The save block is unchanged: the low 16 bits stay in the encrypted `coins` field, the high 16 bits live in the two spare bytes at
0x9C2 (plain). A save from before this update reads 0 there, so every old balance is unchanged (7,777 stays 7,777); a newer save read by an older build shows the low half. The Slot Machine
and the Roulette are the GBA's (four digits, stop at 9,999): a table takes the wallet whole below 9,999 coins and a **5,000-coin stake** from 9,999 up, and puts the stake and the winnings back
on leaving; the rest waits in reserve, and the save is not touched until you leave the table (as before).

  instead of 200). The slot machines and the roulette remain the faster route for a player who wins.
* **Mega Ring:** the free gift from Flannery with the 4th badge is gone on the PC game; a ring a player already owns stays theirs, is never sold again and costs nothing. The dealer sells nothing without
  the ring and says where to buy it. The Mega Stone shop is the game's own shop screen with the prices and your balance in coins ("COINS" instead of "MONEY"); the POKeNEWS sale does not apply.
* **Stone prices are deterministic** (`tools/mp/gen_mega_prices.py`): restricted legendary Mega 100,000; sub-legendary or Mythical Mega 90,000; Mega base stat total 700: 80,000; 620-699: 60,000;

* **Economy audit.** Coin sources: the Slot Machine and the Roulette (the GBA's tables, whose rate depends on the machine's bias, the reel-time minigame and the player; **not measured in this
  update**), buying with money (20 yen a coin, measured by the economy check), and nothing else. The Charm's 100,000 coins therefore cost a player who does not gamble 2,000,000 yen; the Mega Ring 500,000 yen;
  the cheapest stone 600,000 yen; the dearest 2,000,000 yen. The adjustments made are the minimum the new prices need and are all documented here: the 32-bit wallet, the table stake, the 5,000-coin pack (same

## 7. Multiplayer (part 7)

* **Who generates what.** Offline overworld and personal encounters are generated by each player's own game. In a world that shares wild Pokemon the host generates the visible land spawns
  (`spawn_director.py`) and the games only draw them and claim them (`multiplayer_spawns.c`); surfing, fishing, Rock Smash, the Safari Zone and the Pokemon Tower stay personal.
* **Legendary/Mythical on shared spawns:** the host rolls the 1-in-6,000 chance **once, when a player first claims a spawn** (`SpawnDirector.roll_legend_on_claim`), not when it appears, so a player cannot
  pick which spawn to walk to by its species. A hit turns the slot into a Legendary/Mythical of that map's habitat pool at the level and with the shiny status the slot already had. The roll is made once per
  Pokemon (`legend_rolled` is saved with the slot): a release, a disconnect and reconnect, another player's claim or a host restart never rolls it again. Four players claiming their own spawns each roll once; nothing is
  multiplied by the number of players. The pool, area tags and weather tags are the game's own (`scripts/multiplayer/check_legend_pool.py` compares the host's pool with the game's for 2,516 map/weather/time combinations: 0 differences).
* **Shiny Charm on a shared spawn:** the host rolls the spawn's shiny status once (1/4096) when it appears. The claimant's Charm adds two extra rolls, made in the claimant's game from a hash of the
  spawn's id and personality (`MultiplayerSpawns_CharmMakesShiny`): exactly 8191/4096^2, and the same Pokemon always gives the same answer, so releasing and claiming it again, reconnecting, or two
  players with Charms can reroll nothing. A player without the Charm gets only the host's roll. 20,000,000 Pokemon: 9,901 extra shinies (expected 9,764 +- 99); total 1 in 1365.7 with the host's roll.
* **Safari-only families** are never chosen for a shared spawn; the parity check (`check_engine_spawn_weights.py`: 923,520 weights, 0 differences) covers it.
* **Persistence and compatibility:** shared slots, worlds and saves keep their formats (older spawn state loads with `legend_rolled` false); no protocol change; the wallet is save-compatible both ways as above.

## 8. Tests

Engine native: `--selftest-legend` (60 checks), `--selftest-shiny` (12), `--selftest-coins` (41), plus the existing targets (see the ledger). Host: `tests/test_legend_spawns.py`, `scripts/multiplayer/check_legend_pool.py`,
`scripts/multiplayer/check_engine_spawn_weights.py`. In-game scripted checks: `tools/pc_port/economy_check.py` (14 scenarios through the real prize counter, dealer and coin counter scripts, in 3D and Classic 2D).
Boundary/error cases covered: empty pool, unknown species/weather/map, the Battle Pyramid/Pike, a stale or foreign note, a never-hit roll, a forced hit, wallet edges (0, 9,999, 65,535, 65,536, 999,999, over the cap),
a save from before the update, a changed save encryption key, a wallet that cannot pay, full bag, no Coin Case, Mega Ring or Charm already owned, no room for coins.

## 9. Species data safety

`tools/pc_port/species_data_check.py` (make target `check-species-data`): `--dump-species-data` hashes 1,571 species' base stats, types, abilities, catch rate, growth rate, EVs, gender, egg data, friendship,
level-up/TM/egg learnsets and evolutions and compares them with the baseline committed from the pre-update engine; it also fails if a protected data file (species/move/ability/trainer data, the wild
encounter tables, constants, map scripts) differs from the pre-update commit unless a reviewed exception is listed (only the Game Corner coin counter script is). Result: 1,571 of 1,571 identical, no protected file changed.
The GBA ROM is byte-identical (sha256 `f214735b...3387`): every change is PC-only.

## 10. Not verified / limits

Real-PC and real-GPU behaviour (nothing here ran on a GPU); the look of the native Game Corner and shop screens with 6-digit coins in 3D (the logic and the scripts were run, the layout was not inspected on a real display);
real keyboard/controller; real LAN/Internet multiplayer with two PCs (the host logic is unit-tested and the engine side is self-tested, not played across a network); the Slot Machine and Roulette stake
with the actual minigames played to the end; the slot machine's real payout rate; a Safari Zone run from the Mauville Game Corner (the Zone's encounter code is exercised by the generator test, not by walking in).
bios.c licensing is unresolved and the package is not cleared for distribution.
