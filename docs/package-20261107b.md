## What changed since run 365's build

Engine `6d46a38d` to `a4a318ec` (the PC 3D view only, the GBA ROM unchanged); the app's `9e9524f` (the launcher only).
What a player meets:

* **The land boulders' foot (MS-102's last part, cosmetic)** (engine `a4a318ec`): in the 3D view the boulders on the
  sand and the grass (210 on 23 maps: Dewford, Routes 111 and 118 among them) showed grey specks round their foot, the
  General tileset's grey rim that the GBA draws where the rock meets the ground. That grey is now out of the model's
  drawing, as it was taken out of the sea stacks', so the ground the map paints under the boulder shows there. Only that
  model changed (L: every model and room the generator makes proved exact; pictures before and after). Nothing of
  MS-102 is left.
* **A save the host could not store is said as such (MS-214, Low)** (app `9e9524f`): when the host's disk is full, a
  player's save to the world is refused, and nothing is lost (the world keeps the previous save; the player's PC keeps
  the unsent one and sends the same bytes again when the world is reopened). The player was told only "This save needs
  recovery"; the launcher and the in-game session window now say "The host's PC could not store this save (its disk
  may be full). Your progress is kept on this PC: close the game and reopen the world through Host Game or Join Game
  once the host has space.", and the in-game save's banner "Host PC could not store it. / Kept on this PC: reopen
  later." Nothing about saving itself changed. Found by filling a real host's disk to its last byte (L: TEST_LEDGER
  "Save integrity: the host's disk full").

## Not in this build

No engine commit after `a4a318ec`. The app's `8a4b96d` (MS-215, the launcher only), in the full run [369](https://github.com/kashkevin/personal-pokemon-game-emgba3d/actions/runs/42356351245) (dispatched 08:38 UTC, the update check from this run's installer):
when the disk of the PC that plays is full, a save to a world cannot write its journal, so nothing is sent and nothing is
damaged; the launcher stopped saving and said "This save needs recovery. Close the game and reopen", which would have
ended the progress only the running game held. Now that save is refused definitely and saving goes on: the player is
told to keep the game open and free some space ("This PC could not store the save (its disk may be full)..."), the
autosave tries again a minute later, and the host-loss saver forgets an export the game let go (L: a real full disk
before and after, TEST_LEDGER "Save integrity: the player's own disk full"). In this build the old message stands; the
case needs the player's own disk full.
