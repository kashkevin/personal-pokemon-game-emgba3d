# The Game Corner's item hub (2026-10-31)

The owner's request of 2026-10-31 ("complete item expansion", the Game Corner as the hub in ten kinds, every item working,
prices shown before they are applied, existing prices and the casino unchanged, a separate Expansion features report) and
answers (`docs/owner-decisions.md` 30-31: "dont change existing price ... make items cost appropriate if their good items
not cheap"; the Master Ball 200,000, Rare Candy 5,000, Bottle Cap 2,500, Ability Capsule 5,000; then the revised table:
"yes approve"). Engine `cc78b9e9` (PC only: the GBA ROM's sha256 is unchanged), app (this change). The Expansion features report is
`docs/expansion-features-20261031.md`.

**L** below means the Linux test builds (the 3D one with Mesa's software OpenGL, and the Classic 2D one); **W** means the
Windows CI's GitHub-hosted runners (windows-2022), not a player's PC. Nothing here was run on a player's PC.

* The eight lists of the hub's own are the **249 RARE GOODS** (items no player can get anywhere else, since 2026-10-24),
  each at its own price, unchanged, and **199 items found elsewhere in the game** at the owner's approved prices (the full
  list is at the end). The Mega Stone dealer and the TM counter still sell as before; the hub opens their lists too.
* **No price sold today changed**: the RARE GOODS, the dealer's 82 stones, the five TM prizes, the Mega Ring, the Shiny
  Charm, the dolls, and the casino's bets and payouts.
* **The Master Ball** costs 200,000 coins and can be bought again and again (the owner: "buy as many as you like"); a full
  wallet (999,999) buys four.
* **The Town Map** (1,000) is one per save, SOLD OUT once owned; it is how Fly from the map (2026-10-31) becomes
  reachable, besides the PokéNav's HOENN MAP.
* Every item in the hub is a real item that works here; key items show SOLD OUT once owned, as everywhere.

## The prices (approved 2026-10-31)

1. Every price the Game Corner or the Mega Stone dealer had stays exactly as it was.
2. The owner's own: Master Ball 200,000 (repeat purchases), Ability Capsule 5,000, Rare Candy 5,000, Bottle Cap 2,500.
3. Everyday consumables at a mart's price at the coin clerk's 20 money a coin (money / 20), or less where the corner
   already sold the same effect cheaper (the vitamins 300, as the Mochi that give the same +10 EVs).
4. An item that works exactly like one sold before costs the same (Scope Lens = Razor Claw 1,500; type boosters = their
   Plate, 3,000).
5. Everything else by how good it is, on the TM tiers: 10,000 competitive staples (Choice Band, Specs and Scarf, Life
   Orb, Leftovers, Assault Vest), 7,500 strong (Focus Sash, Rocky Helmet, Expert Belt, Black Sludge), 5,000 good but
   narrower (Weakness Policy, Lum Berry, Destiny Knot, Light Ball), 2,500 situational, 1,000 niche; one tier lower for an
   item used up when it works. Nothing new costs more than 10,000 but the Master Ball.

As approved: the **Psychic Seed** is on sale at 750 like the other Seeds although nothing here sets Psychic Terrain (it
never activates); the **Floettite** stays at the dealer although nothing here can Mega Evolve with it; **Rare Candy**
costs 5,000 although an Exp. Candy XL (1,500) is usually the better buy.

## Every item works

The item audit of 2026-10-27 (`docs/item-audit-20261027.md`, `docs/item-audit-20261027-items.md`) checked every item of
the game's list. Of the 199 items the hub adds: **164** by tests (the self-tests of balls, healing, bag items, held items,
berries, evolution and form items, and the expansion's GBA battle tests of hold effects), **16** by the game's own code
(an NPC who takes the item, the item's use from the Bag), **19** inferred (the contest scarves, the Pokeblock-only
berries, the flutes and the Fluffy Tail and Poké Doll); none was found broken. Not for sale, with their reasons (the
item proposal's count of the expansion's 873 items, updated): Z-Move, Dynamax and Terastallization items (60: those
gimmicks are off on PC), items for Gen 7-9 species nobody can own here (55), placeholders (4: three Legends Z-A foods and
the e-Reader Enigma Berry; TM51-TM100 are TMs of the catalogue since 2026-10-31), key and story items (28), FireRed /
LeafGreen and Orre key items no script uses (18), items with no effect in this engine (14: ores, sweets, Aux items),
fossils nobody revives (13), treasures with no use but selling (10), mulch (8: off), the HMs (8), Apricorns (7: no ball
maker), mail (7), key items no source gives (5), the Beast Ball (the owner's decision of 2026-10-27), the Exp. Share (no
effect on PC: the party's share is always on), the Leader's Crest and the Peat Block (no use here).
