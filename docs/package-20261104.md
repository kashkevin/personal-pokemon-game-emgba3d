## What changed since run 265's build

* **Eight-place Championships with computer trainers** (step 18, the owner's decision of 2026-11-03): proposed and
  accepted in the Players menu, the bracket, computer matches as the Tower's solo command, the final, the champion and
  the Trainer Card's counters; battles between players after ordinary battles no longer stop at their intro (MS-180).
* **The last GBA screens in the 3D UI** (step 15c): the minigames with computer partners, the cable car's ride, every
  dynmultichoice menu (the lobbies, the Game Corner's counters).
* **The 3D world's long tail** (step 16): Ever Grande's halls climb to their landings; the Battle Factory's and Battle
  Palace's lobbies and sixteen other furnished rooms cut by hand, the bike shop's furniture (MS-164); the Battle
  Pyramid's top and the Contest Hall's stages on their levels; Jagged Pass a staircase of levels (MS-153); Route 119's
  hills and long bridge; the Safari Zone's halves meet on one level; Fortree's and Mossdeep's trees stand (MS-172); a
  person under a cave's bridge stands under its deck (MS-186); stairs that stepped instead of climbing (MS-187).
* **The crash hunt's fixes** (MS-169): a warp's last frame no longer mixes two maps (MS-182); doors' and the Mega
  indicator's palettes no longer read past their lists (MS-183, MS-184); the PC screens' copies of game strings stop at
  the string's end (MS-185); a cancelled save command no longer stops the bridge (MS-12); a game's first World Event
  action is never skipped (M21).
* **The same version for everyone** (MS-28, the owner's choice): a launcher of another version is refused at join with
  "Different game versions".
* **Divers shown diving** (MS-181): another player under the water is drawn swimming there, also in their menu.
