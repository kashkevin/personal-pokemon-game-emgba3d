## What changed since run 288's build

* **World trades** (KNOWN_ISSUES MS-194): a trade takes every form a Gen 6 game trades, 163 of them, each named in the
  menus (a female Meowstic, Burmy's and Wormadam's cloaks, East Sea Shellos and Gastrodon, Blue-Striped Basculin, the
  seasons, patterns, colours and trims, Pumpkaboo's sizes, Deoxys's formes, Origin Giratina, Sky Shaymin, Arceus's
  types, the Therian Formes, Resolute Keldeo, Genesect's drives, Unbound Hoopa). Before, every form but Rotom's was
  refused ("No.1155" in the list). A Pumpkaboo of any size evolves into the Gourgeist of its size when traded. Eggs and
  Mail are still refused; their options are in KNOWN_ISSUES ("Trades: eggs and Mail").
* **The Championships' rules** (MS-193): Species Clause and No Legendaries count a form as its species (two Rotom forms
  were two species; Origin Giratina, Deoxys's formes and the other legendary forms passed No Legendaries).
* **Save integrity**: the world server's process killed in the middle of a save (a crashed launcher, End task) keeps
  every save it acknowledged, byte for byte (a new test, also on Windows); and a world that cannot be opened at that
  moment (a lock held elsewhere, an I/O error, a full disk) is no longer reported as damaged (MS-195).
* **Invite codes** (NU-7): one friend's code can be replaced, the old one refused at once, the friend's account and
  saves kept.
* **The 3D view at map seams** (MS-118), each change drawing exactly what it drew before: the buildings' sync compares
  with what it last uploaded (95-98 ms instead of 239-243 ms over the seam walk); a chunk's signature is hashed four
  times faster; an atlas redraw clears only the rows it uploads; and a chunk whose cells changed only in their gameplay
  elevation, which the mesh never uses, is no longer built again (the seam walk's chunk builds 412-415 to 368-374).
