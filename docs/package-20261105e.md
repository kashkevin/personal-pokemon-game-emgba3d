## What changed since run 322's build

Engine `fb6bcd9f` to `8765e3ac` (20 commits; every change PC only, the GBA ROM unchanged) and app `cbc4419` to `2aecaea`
(44 commits). What a player meets:

* **Saving and multiplayer**
  * **World Events (G3, owner decision #38):** a World Event Pokemon is reserved for five minutes after its claimant
    disconnects; the claimant can reconnect and reclaim it then; it is released afterwards; one claim at a time (was
    120 seconds).
  * **Trades of Eggs and of Pokemon holding Mail (owner decision #39):** Mail moves with its Pokemon inside the trade's
    own two post-images (journal and recovery cover it); an Egg trades as its exact record and, hatched, makes its
    hatcher its original trainer, re-keyed (before the engine fix it became a Bad Egg); the host accepts exactly that
    one change and refuses a returning copy of the unhatched Egg.
  * **World loading (MS-161):** each save's checked reading is remembered beside the world, keyed by the save's own
    SHA-256 and the reader's code, and checked again when either changes; every replay and integrity check still runs.
  * **Legendary Tickets (owner decision #40):** the island tickets from postgame achievements, challenging quests and
    Battle Frontier milestones (no casino source), given by the host as a reward word (kind 8) with their flags, once
    each; PLAYERS > World and my base > Frontier and League > Legendary Tickets shows them.
  * **Location privacy (owner decision #47):** "Share my location with other players" off by default (launcher and
    in-game menu); a private player's place is shown to nobody but a viewer on the same map.
  * **The Battle Tower's screens (MS-52):** the native Tower screens draw the host's record sent with the page.
  * **Controller B (owner decision #43):** checked: B answers No / Back on every multiplayer confirmation, never
    confirms (no behaviour change).
* **3D**
  * **MS-118:** no drawing under a fully covered fade, the builds that may wait spread over frames by time, the nearest
    map's border past the edge (the pop gone), atlas regions that stay put over a crossing.
  * **3D vehicles (owner decision #44):** the S.S. Tidal sails past its porthole in 3D.
  * **MS-154:** Route 118's river head lies with its river; none of its ledges lands a level higher.
  * **Battle animations (VR-5):** HD versions of the 16 moves past the Battle FX package a player can meet, and 168 more.
* **Fixes**

    room loads (found by the MS-98 sweep).
  * **MS-198:** the AI's damage estimate no longer reads past the critical-hit odds table for a target that blocks
    critical hits (no visible change in these builds).
* **Audio (owner decision #45):** master, music, sound effect and Pokemon cry volumes (music 70% and effects 100% by
