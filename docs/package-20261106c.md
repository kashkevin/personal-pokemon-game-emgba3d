## What changed since run 344's build

Engine `3a873f8c` to `a5738d3b` (every change PC only, the GBA ROM unchanged); the app's one change a player meets is
the battle observer's. What a player meets:

* **Mr. Briney's sails and the sea in 3D (MS-82)**: a tileset coming into view by a neighbouring map draws its region of
  the atlas alone instead of the whole atlas (1.5-2.4 ms against 5-9 ms), and slots of the sea's animated tiles left an
  animation step behind after their cells came back into view are drawn again (engine `4ade4ae4`, `0064d13b`; the
  atlas check on Windows in run 346: 84,590 slots, none differed).
* **The Battle Frontier's transition (MS-108)**: its logo and circles drawn as true circles at the window's resolution
  (engine `39f3a8e3`, `cd0a4510`).
* **The Battle Pyramid's top in 3D battles (E5)**: the stage stands on the summit, not before a tier's face (engine
  `39c67075`).
* **A Battle Tower floor in a slow game (MS-49)**: a queued solo floor is refused after 45 seconds of real time too, not
  only of frames (engine `5b18ebf1`).
* **Team Magma's hideout in 3D (E9)**: its machines stand as boxes as drawn, the long machine and the dome and drill
  rigs too, and no battler stands on one (engine `5d9c2aa4`, `7a8746f2`).
* **The small sea stacks in 3D (MS-102)**: their foot meets the water without a ring of grey (engine `a5738d3b`).
* **A battle between players when a PC stops a game for seconds (MS-165)**: the launcher's battle observer waits as long
  for the game's answer to its look as for a host command, so a command handed over during the stop no longer reaches
  the stopped game behind it (app `7edaed9`; found by Windows run 349, the fix passed on Windows in run 352).
