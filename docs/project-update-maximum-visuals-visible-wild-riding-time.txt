# PROJECT UPDATE — MAXIMUM FAKE-2.5D VISUALS + VISIBLE WILD POKÉMON + RIDING + DAY/NIGHT

Add the following systems to the existing Pokémon Emerald Multiplayer project.

This is an ADDITION to the existing specification.

Do NOT change any other established gameplay rule unless specifically overridden below.

---

# MAXIMUM-QUALITY FAKE 2.5D VISUAL DIRECTION

Keep Pokémon Emerald/pokeemerald-expansion as our technical 2D foundation.

DO NOT attempt to convert the engine into true polygonal 2.5D/3D.

Instead, push the existing 2D engine as visually far as reasonably possible while maintaining multiplayer stability and acceptable performance.

The target is:

A highly enhanced, atmospheric, depth-heavy Pokémon pixel-art world that visually takes inspiration from later Pokémon games and HD-2D presentation while remaining technically compatible with our Emerald-based architecture.

Research existing compatible graphics/tileset/rendering enhancements before building everything from scratch.

- Up to 4 customized players
- Up to 4 follower Pokémon

Design a sensible visual/performance budget.

Investigate systems such as:

- Maximum active overworld Pokémon
- Effect limits
- Animation distance
- Reduced animation/update rates for distant entities
- Culling
- Sprite/object limits
- Simplified distant effects

Prioritize stable gameplay over one additional visual effect.

# VISIBLE WILD POKÉMON

Change the primary wild encounter presentation.

Instead of relying primarily on:

Walk through grass
→ invisible random encounter

wild Pokémon should generally be VISIBLE in the overworld.

Pokémon should physically appear in appropriate environments.

Examples:

Land Pokémon walking through grass/terrain.

Bird Pokémon appearing in appropriate outdoor environments.

Water Pokémon visibly swimming where technically practical.

Rare Pokémon visibly appearing as rare spawns.

The player approaches/touches/interacts with the Pokémon to initiate the battle.

---

# RANDOMIZATION STILL EXISTS

Our random Pokémon system remains.

The difference is PRESENTATION.

Instead of secretly rolling the species only after entering grass, the spawn system determines which Pokémon appears and creates its overworld representation.

The existing rules remain:

- Gen 1–6 species pool
- No ordinary wild Legendary/Mythical Pokémon
- Appropriate progression
- Mostly basic/first-stage Pokémon early
- Rarity weighting
- Shinies
- Starters may appear
- Strong families can appear rarely at low/basic stages

---

# SHINY OVERWORLD POKÉMON

Where technically practical, shiny Pokémon should appear SHINY in the overworld.

This means a player may actually notice:

SHINY POKÉMON!

before initiating battle.

Do not hide the shiny appearance until battle if our sprite/assets architecture can support overworld shiny coloration reliably.

This should make overworld shiny hunting exciting.

---

# SHARED VISIBLE SPAWNS

Because Pokémon physically exist in the shared world, investigate making visible wild Pokémon shared world entities.

If a rare Beldum appears near multiple connected players, those players should be able to see the same Beldum.

Recommended initial behavior:

FIRST PLAYER TO INITIATE THE ENCOUNTER CLAIMS THE SPAWN TEMPORARILY.

Other players cannot steal the Pokémon while the encounter is active.

Determine appropriate behavior if the initiating player:

- Catches it
- Defeats it
- Runs away
- Disconnects
- Crashes

Prevent duplication/desynchronization.

Document the final spawn ownership rules before implementation.

---

# OPTIONAL TRADITIONAL ENCOUNTERS

Do not permanently remove the possibility of traditional hidden/random encounters at the engine level.

Keep the architecture flexible enough that certain areas, modes, special mechanics, or future settings could still use traditional encounters if desired.

However:

VISIBLE OVERWORLD POKÉMON should become our primary normal encounter presentation.

# RIDEABLE POKÉMON

Add a Pokémon riding/mount system.

IMPORTANT:

RIDING IS PRIMARILY A GROUND MOVEMENT/COSMETIC SYSTEM.

It is NOT automatically connected to Fly, Surf, or other traversal abilities.

Example:

Player rides Charizard.

Charizard travels along the ground as a mount.

This does NOT automatically allow the player to fly.

---

# RIDE ELIGIBILITY

Pokémon that are physically appropriate and have supported assets/animations may eventually be rideable.

Examples may include:

- Arcanine
- Rapidash
- Tauros
- Rhyhorn
- Rhydon
- Charizard
- Tyranitar
- Salamence
- Garchomp
- Zebstrika
- Sawsbuck
- Other sufficiently large/appropriate Pokémon

Do not assume every Pokémon must immediately support riding.

Create a supported ride roster and expand it over time.

---

# RIDE BALANCE

Do NOT create a situation where everyone chooses the same Pokémon because it has the highest hidden movement speed.

Prefer standardized or tightly balanced riding speeds.

Mount choice should primarily be:

COSMETIC/PERSONAL PREFERENCE

rather than competitive optimization.

A modest movement-speed improvement over normal walking/running is acceptable.

Exact speed should be balanced later.

---

# FOLLOWER → MOUNT

Where supported, allow a player's current follower Pokémon to transition naturally into a mount.

Example:

Arcanine is following Player.

Player interacts/selects Mount.

Mount animation occurs.

Player rides Arcanine.

Player dismounts.

Arcanine returns to follower state.

This should feel seamless where technically practical.

---

# MULTIPLAYER RIDING

Other connected players must see:

- Which Pokémon you are riding
- Your mounted movement
- Mount/dismount state
- Appropriate animations

Do NOT make mounts visible only on the local player's client.

Mount state becomes part of synchronized player appearance/state.

---

# RIDING DOES NOT EQUAL TRAVERSAL ABILITY

Keep these systems separate.

Riding Charizard:
Does NOT automatically unlock Fly.

Riding a Pokémon capable of swimming:
Does NOT automatically mean Surf is unlocked unless our separate traversal rules allow it.

Mounting is its own system.

Traversal abilities are separate gameplay/progression systems.

# POKÉMON EMERALD — COMPLETE GRAPHICS, SPRITES & ANIMATION UPGRADE

## YOUR MISSION

We are developing a heavily upgraded Pokémon Emerald multiplayer game with Gen 6 mechanics, Pokémon from Generations 1–6, Mega Evolutions, visible wild Pokémon, following Pokémon, rideable Pokémon, a day/night cycle, and an ambitious fake-2.5D graphical style inspired by Octopath Traveler.

Your mission is to dramatically improve the entire game's visual presentation, character animations, Pokémon sprites, overworld animations, battle effects, environments, and movement systems.

**CRITICAL: DO NOT RECREATE ASSETS FROM SCRATCH WHEN HIGH-QUALITY EXISTING ASSETS ARE AVAILABLE.**

Pokémon Emerald has existed since 2004. The ROM-hacking community has spent more than 20 years creating upgraded sprites, animations, graphics, and systems.

Research these resources extensively, find the best available implementations, and use them as our starting foundation.

However, **do not treat existing community assets as the quality ceiling.** If you can improve their appearance, animation smoothness, consistency, lighting, detail, or overall presentation, you are encouraged to do so.

Our goal is not simply to make Emerald look slightly better. Our goal is to make it look and feel like an exceptionally polished modern Pokémon game while preserving its pixel-art identity.
