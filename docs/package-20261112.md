## What changed since run 437's build

The engine's `4e83636a` (the 3D view only, PC only: `buildings.c`, how a building model's own cells are laid under it);
the app unchanged but for records; the GBA ROM unchanged. What a player meets:

* **No sky through three building models' gaps (KNOWN_ISSUES MS-240, Low: the 3D view)** (engine `4e83636a`): a
  building model's cells are laid with a ground taken from the cells round it, and where the 3D view stands the 3DS
  trees it draws a tree's crown top as grass, so no cell in view kept that metatile and the 3D view's atlas never drew
  it: three of the 5,043 models had such a ground and drew nothing between their parts, the sky showing through - one
  of Fortree City's tree houses (its east one, under its canopy: 46 pixels at the geometry check's camera), a model at
  Rustboro (10 pixels) and the Pokemon League's roof at Ever Grande (1 pixel). Such a ground is now laid as the view
  draws it (grass). With the Diorama's voxel trees (F8) the three show no sky either.

Play is unchanged: the change only draws.
