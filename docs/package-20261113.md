## What changed since run 444's build

* **The default trees whole at one height (KNOWN_ISSUES MS-243, the owner's decision #54)** (engine `7da6a174`): a large
  tree whose cells the 3D rules put on different levels stood split - its upper quarters a level above its lower ones;
  each stands whole now at its highest quarter's height, its trunk and art carried down to each lower quarter's ground,
  the terrain unchanged.
* **Shoal Cave's low-tide bridges (MS-186, #55)** (engine `e4cdc926`): a layout of the room's own - both decks on the
  walkways' level, the ground under them lower, the stairs climbing as the art draws them, a player under a deck seen
  through it; walking, collision and the tide unchanged.
* **Mt. Pyre's stairwell and its likes (MS-237, #56)** (engine `4be27e1b`): the stairwells modelled by hand - the frame
  one wall over the open doorway, its recess closed, the taller side walls carried on to it; the walls keep their
  heights; at Mt. Pyre 1F-5F and the 5 like them (Team Aqua's hideout, the Space Center).
* **The cramped rooms' battle backgrounds (V10, #57)** (engine `1786e968`): where no stage near the player is clear in a
  room or a cave, the battle stands in an arena of the room's own (its floor, its north wall behind and round the
  battle, its commonest object along the sides, its light); elsewhere, and in the Frontier's and the Battle Tents'
  battle rooms, the room as it is.
* The Windows checks: the 3D job's tour gains Shoal Cave's two places and Mt. Pyre 5F's (34 places); a step with two
  battles, one that must take the arena and one that must not.

Play is unchanged: the changes only draw.
