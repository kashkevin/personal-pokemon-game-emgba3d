## What changed since run 338's build

Engine `0397596d` to `3a873f8c` (every change PC only, the 3D view only, the GBA ROM unchanged); the app's commits are
proofs, the workflow and records (nothing a player meets). What a player meets:

* **Mr. Briney's sails in 3D (MS-82)**: a step of the sea's animation no longer builds every chunk waiting for its build
  at once (up to seven in a frame before); a chunk waiting off screen is built within the time budget, one in view as
  before (engine `c81da595`). On GitHub's Windows runner (Mesa's software renderer) the sail from Dewford to Route 104
  had no terrain sync over 16 ms (3 a sail before), the longest 7.4 ms (20-21 ms before). And a chunk now stands as
  the grid moves only if the cells a sign or a jump ledge at its edge reads are unchanged too (engine `05ffdc33`: a
  stale chunk the terrain check found, its mesh the same).
* **The rooms' doors (V6)**: the doors no building model stands on - the lifts, the S.S. Tidal's cabins, corridors -
  open flat on their drawing, as the outdoor doors on modelled buildings already did (engine `3a873f8c`).
* **The sea stacks' foot (MS-102)**: the General rock's grey rim lies with the ground instead of standing as a pale band
  at the sea stacks' foot (engine `08d3c9f5`).
