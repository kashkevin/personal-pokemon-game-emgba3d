## What changed since run 476's build

* **The Acro Bike's bumpy slopes in four-pixel steps** (the owner's #71 B; engine `0fcd61b1`, the 3D view only): Jagged
  Pass's and the Safari Zone North's bumpy slopes climb in the stairs' four stone steps a cell instead of sixteen
  one-pixel slats; the Mach Bike's muddy slopes keep their one-pixel steps; the player's height at a cell's centre is
  unchanged.
* **Two of the four dotted seams** (the object audit's class 6, the owner's #73; engine `13ea1413`, the building tools
  only): the S.S. Tidal's cabin line and the Ranking Hall's black corner - a back wall's void runs a pixel on over the
  room's outside. (The Rustboro table seam was already gone with `5c2140b1`; Wanda's rug, the fourth, is engine
  `1334a605`, in the next build.)
* **The Acro Bike rails stand on their posts** (the owner's #73; engine `6514bb97`, the 3D view only): the Safari Zone
  South's rails show a front under the beam and posts down to the ground from a low camera instead of a thin deck.
  Route 119's rails stay flat (the owner's choice to make, #78).
* **The PC's old copies of world saves** (MS-216, the owner's #72 B; app `032613e`, `c320be0`, the launcher only):
  restoring a character from a kept copy checked first; then the launcher keeps the newest 40 copies of each world
  account's saves (with their receipts) and removes older ones. Every save inside the world itself is kept.

Play is unchanged: the 3D changes only draw; collision, warps and events are the GBA game's; MS-216 removes only the
PC's spare copies.
