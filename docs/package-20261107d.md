## What changed since run 369's build

The engine's `18e66680` and `50d865c5` (PC only); the app's code and the GBA ROM unchanged. What a player meets:

* **Another player under the water is drawn on the Pokemon that dove (MS-181's last part, Low)** (engine `18e66680`
  with the review's fixes `50d865c5`; PC only, the GBA ROM unchanged; no app change): another player who dove was shown
  with the original game's underwater look (the plain diver with the generic blob drawn in), while each game draws its
  own diver on the silhouette of the Pokemon that dove. Now a game under the water publishes the Pokemon that carries
  its player, as on the water, and every other game draws that player as its own diver: their character's surfing pose
  as the silhouette, on their carrier's silhouette. The carrier is made only when every sprite palette it needs is free
  and never takes one from anything else (the wild Pokemon on the field keep their colours); without a free palette the
  original's look stays and it is tried again a second later; the diver and their carrier come and go together, so a
  rider is never drawn on nothing. Each game's own diver is drawn as before.
