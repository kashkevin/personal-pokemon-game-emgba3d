## What changed since run 438's build

* **F12's pictures where the player can find them (KNOWN_ISSUES MS-241)** (engine `dbe0b439`, `2ff61e87`; app
  `3f4e8e9`): in a 3D game the launcher starts, F12's picture goes to the player's Pictures folder's EmeraldTogether
  folder (wherever Pictures is: moved, or in OneDrive), not into a new hidden launch folder each time; the notice names
  the folder (a part with letters past ASCII, as "Imágenes", left out: the game's font has none).
* **The 3D view's large trees (KNOWN_ISSUES V23)** (engine `a9038dab`, `5e4dd696`): with the default (3DS) trees a tree
  row whose lower row stands half a tree to the side drew those quarters the wrong way round (189 cells: Petalburg Woods,
  Petalburg City, Rustboro, Lilycove, Routes 104, 115, 116 and 117, the Safari Zone, Southern Island); 23 trees at a
  bank's edge stood as flat-topped hedges and stand whole now; a tree's crown tips over water show the water at its
  level. With the voxel trees (F8) 188 tree blocks and 111 tip rows the rules had no pattern for lay as plain ground,
  the trees' art painted flat - Petalburg Woods' band among them; every large tree of the General tileset stands as a
  tree now.
* **The voxel trees' banks (KNOWN_ISSUES MS-242, the F8 option only)** (engine `e6c15824`): with the Diorama's voxel
  trees the sky showed through the bank under a row of trees where it meets a pond or a lower terrace; the bank reads the
  ground now, rock a level tall as the 3DS trees' banks. The default 3DS trees are untouched.
* **MS-240's second part** (engine `622a3f3f`): its rule (a building model's tree ground laid as grass) reads the tree
  style the snapshot's cells were made with, so F8 pressed in a running game leaves no sky for a frame.
* The Windows checks: the 3D job's tour gains MS-240's three places (32), the sky through gaps counted in its pictures,
  MS-241's notices read, and six of MS-242's places drawn with the voxel trees; the shiny check's roamer case bumps the
  roamer once it has stood on screen 80 frames and reads its twinkles there (test builds only).

Play is unchanged: the changes only draw, choose where a picture is saved, or are the checks' own.
