## What changed since run 427's build

The engine's `4b9cf4e3`, `efac4985`, `01eb62cc`, `ef24627e` and `18c53566` (the 3D view only, PC only: how the building
generator stands a room's pieces, and how the terrain draws one kind of cell's side); the app's `8a64069` (the launcher);
the GBA ROM unchanged. What a player meets:

* **The Battle Palace battle room's back wall whole (KNOWN_ISSUES MS-235, Low: the 3D view)** (engine `4b9cf4e3`): its
  two outer pillars stood out a row before the wall and its door's posts a row behind it, a black wedge beside each,
  in the room and in its battles, where the 2D game draws the wall whole. The pillars and the door now lie on the back
  wall with the middle pillars.
* **Lift and cabin doors standing in their walls (MS-236, Low: the 3D view)** (engines `efac4985` and `01eb62cc`): the
  Battle Tower lobby's four lifts, the S.S. Tidal's four cabin doors and the Abandoned Ship's six lay back under black
  between upright posts and opened flat; they now stand in their walls and open on them, as an outdoor building's door
  does. The last 4 of the 18 doors, the Battle Pike's curtain in the back wall of its normal room and its room of wild
  Pokemon, now stand with the wall, black over them before; while the curtain moves it lies back as before.
* **The Sootopolis Gym 1F's back wall level (MS-237, Low: the 3D view)** (engine `ef24627e`): its two waterfalls and its
  side walls stood taller than the wall between them, a black notch beside the waterfalls and a strip beside the side
  walls; they now stand as tall as the wall. Mt. Pyre 5F's thin lines beside its stairwell (the same kind) are left as
  they are, a design call (options in KNOWN_ISSUES MS-237).
* **The Battle Palace pools' line of sky (MS-239, Low: the 3D view)** (engine `18c53566`): the side of a cell drawn
  wholly on its upper layer drew nothing, so a pixel's line of sky showed where the battle room's two sunk pools meet
  the pillars' bases behind them (and 2 pixels at Aqua Hideout 1F). Such a side now shows the cell's whole drawing.
* **No "Abandon battle" while a cancelled entry winds down (MS-238, Medium: multiplayer battles)** (app `8a64069`): when a
  challenge ended before its battle started while a player's Enter was already admitted (the other trainer's Enter
  refused, a cancel, the challenge expired), that player's launcher offered "Abandon battle" for the moment its neutral
  cancel took - and abandoning starts the game again from its host save, losing progress not saved there. The launcher
  now offers nothing to abandon while the cancel runs (abandonment comes back only if the cancel fails, as before) and
  its status reads "admission_canceling" to the cancel's end. Found by Windows run 433's stale-save job.

Play is unchanged by the 3D view's changes: they only draw.
