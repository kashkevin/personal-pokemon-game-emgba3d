## What changed since run 271's build

* **Save integrity.** A Battle Tower milestone Pokemon is no longer lost when the game closes before saving and the
  older save has no room for it: it is owed again (MS-51). A co-op battle's result waits in the save until its script
  has given it, so a game closed or reset in between gives the same experience once when it starts again (MS-190).
  Kept shiny and Legendary wild Pokemon are remembered in the save across restarts (MS-145).
* **Co-op experience** (the owner's instruction): a co-op Gym battle gives experience only with the win that earns
  this player the Gym's badge, so losing again to farm it gives nothing (`docs/owner-decisions.md` 4).
* **The wireless minigames with the world's players** (MS-141c): Berry Crush, Pokemon Jump and Dodrio Berry Picking
  between two to four of a world's players, through a lobby in the Players menu (each also with a world's four).
* **Mega Evolution** (MS-39, MS-38): Mega Rayquaza is reachable (Dragon Ascent from the move relearners); Mega Lucario
  Z's Aura Guard halves damage from moves that make contact.
* **Trades** (G1, the owner's decision): an evolution trade keeps a Pokemon's known post-Gen-6 move as it is.
* **Performance**: opening a world with many saves is about twice as fast (MS-161; 600 saves checked cold in under a
  second on the Windows runner); walking back over a map's seam in 3D builds no chunks again (MS-118: 20 crossings,
  237 chunks built instead of 2,109); and the 3D view's work on each step and at each crossing is lighter, each change
  drawing exactly what it drew before (MS-118): the terrain mesher's sort (10-21% less meshing), the atlas's redraw
  (its drawing 3.7 to 2.3 ms), the cells' rules resolve on every step (2.1-2.2 times faster).
* **The Players menu** (MS-55): a new page opens on its first row, not on the row of the page before.
* **The 3D world**: east and west ramps the right way round (MS-188), Sootopolis's terraces, the Battle Frontier's
  and Mauville's seams (the last land seams meet, MS-162), the Safari Zone's north-west plateau (MS-153).
* **The START menu** (MS-29): PLAYER > RESET and PLAYERS open every time (the menu ends a turn in place).
