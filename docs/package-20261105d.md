  the game's own window) gave the focus to the row under the mouse pointer when the window opened over a mouse at rest,
  or the pointer crossed its edge without moving, so the keyboard's next press and Enter could act on that row instead
  of the screen's own. Only a mouse that moves now takes the focus; clicks, the wheel and keys are unchanged.

  gameplay plan in the 3D game through Mesa (`gameplay-check-3d`), the multiplayer check's give-up and
